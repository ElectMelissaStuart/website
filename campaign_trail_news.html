Meet Melissa on the Campaign Trail

Melissa's been out knocking doors, meeting neighbors, and admiring some of our neighbors' most beautiful gardens.

She's having so much fun meeting everyone and gathering important feedback. All around town, she's been hearing about the priorities of Redmond residents, their ideas for our community, and making sure folks have access to information they need about their local government.

Melissa will visit every Redmond neighborhood this campaign season. If you'd like to host her on your street or in your building, drop her a quick note with your address and preferred date.

Hope to see you on the trail!
